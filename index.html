Our Sweet Journey

From Harit Agrawal's humble beginning in Pune in 1975 to becoming Gujarat's trusted ice cream brand, our journey spans nearly five decades. What started with handmade ice cream using traditional techniques has evolved into FSSAI-certified quality that serves families across Gujarat.
