Our Sweet Journey

1975 – The Humble Beginning in Pune

In 1975, Harit Agrawal began crafting handmade ice cream in Pune using traditional Indian techniques. With no machines and only pure ingredients, he believed ice cream should be made with honesty, patience, and love. Every scoop delivered a taste of home.

1985 – Growing Through Community Love

Harit Ji's ice cream became known locally for its authentic flavors and fair prices. Word spread through families, festivals, and community gatherings—his ice cream became a part of celebrations.

1995 – Legacy Continues in Gujarat

The family business moved from Pune to Palanpur, Gujarat, where Komalbhai Agrawal continued the tradition with the same purity and passion. The brand evolved—and a new identity was born.

2001 – Birth of Meera Ice Cream

With a commitment to quality and tradition, the brand was officially named Meera Ice Cream. Inspired by devotion, honesty, and purity—just like Meera Bai—this name reflected the Agrawal family's values.

2010 – Tradition Meets Innovation

Modern equipment was added to improve consistency and hygiene without compromising on the original taste. New flavors were developed, including regional favorites like Kesar Pista, Sitafal, and Rajbhog.

2015 – Recognized for Quality

Meera Ice Cream won the Best and Healthy Ice Cream Award in Gujarat, a recognition of its pure ingredients and quality manufacturing process.

2018 – FSSAI Approved

Meera Ice Cream became officially FSSAI certified, ensuring every cup and cone meets national safety and hygiene standards. Purity became not just a promise—but a guarantee.

2022 – Expanding Happiness

What started as a small family venture has now grown into a trusted regional ice cream brand. Meera Ice Cream today serves families, events, restaurants, and celebrations across Gujarat—spreading smiles one scoop at a time.
